# Brand Identity & Design System Guide — tsbeh (Flutter Tasbeeh Al Muslim)

## 1. Brand Strategy
- **Brand Vision:** To be the most trusted, reliable, and unobtrusive digital companion for every Muslim, fostering continuous mindfulness of Allah throughout daily routines.
- **Core Values:** Authenticity (Sunnah-based), Respectfulness (Quiet hours, zero intrusive ads), Accessibility, Spiritual Tranquility.
- **Voice & Tone:** Calm, spiritual, reverent, encouraging, and clear.

## 2. Logo & Symbol System
- **Primary Logo:** Located at `assets/images/logo.png` (featured on Android/iOS app icons, splash screen, and home header).
- **Symbolism:** Islamic spiritual tasbeeh motif combined with modern clean typography.
- **Minimum App Icon Resolution:** 1024x1024 px for store submissions, with adaptive square and round variants for Android.

- Full color tokens, Material 3 palettes, typography scales (`Cairo`), and grid metrics are fully documented in [Visual Identity Guide](../Architecture/Visual_Identity.md).
